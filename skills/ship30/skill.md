# Ship 30 for 30 Essay Generation Skill

## Overview
The Ship 30 for 30 skill transforms grounded product & growth insights into a high-impact, skimmable essay (~1,250 words) adhering strictly to digital writing best practices.

## Core Rules & Principles
1. **Strong Hook (The 1-Sentence Opener)**: Grab attention by stating a counter-intuitive truth or painful reality product leaders face.
2. **Narrative Progression**:
   - Section 1: The Problem / The Status Quo Trap (Why traditional approaches fail).
   - Section 2: The Core Insight (Transcript-grounded framework from Lenny's guest).
   - Section 3: Step-by-Step Actionable Playbook (How to execute today).
   - Section 4: The Strategic Takeaway (High-leverage takeaway for leaders).
3. **Skimmability & Micro-Formatting**:
   - Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max).
   - Clear Markdown headers (`##`, `###`).
   - Bulleted / numbered lists for fast scanning.
   - **Selective bolding** on key action verbs and pivotal concepts.
4. **Strict Knowledge Grounding**:
   - Every claim, framework, or quote must derive from retrieved transcript context.
   - Explicitly credit the guest (e.g. Shreyas Doshi, Elena Verna, Marty Cagan, Patrick Campbell).
   - Never invent unsupported metrics or quotes.
5. **Target Word Count**: ~1,250 words.
